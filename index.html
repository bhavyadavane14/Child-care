🚨 When to Seek Emergency Care Immediately:

  • High fever (>104°F / 40°C) unresponsive to medicine
  • Laboured, unusually fast, or grunting breathing
  • Bluish tinge around lips or fingernails
  • Severe dehydration, no tears or wet diapers > 8 hours
  • Convulsions, seizures, or unresponsiveness
